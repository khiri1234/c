How We Work

From Concept
to Completion

A structured methodology refined over 15 years of high-stakes façade installations across the UAE.

01

Site Survey & Engineering

Detailed structural surveys, wind-load calculations, and thermal performance analysis. Every project begins with data, not assumptions.

02

Design & Shop Drawing

Precision CAD shop drawings reviewed against architectural intent — coordinated with structural and MEP consultants before fabrication begins.

03

Custom Fabrication

Aluminum profiles cut, drilled, and assembled in our facility with CNC precision. Glass units specified and sourced from approved UAE suppliers.

04

Installation & QA

Certified installation teams working to BS 8000 and UAE construction standards. Every panel verified for alignment, seal integrity, and water infiltration.

05

Handover & Warranty

Full As-Built documentation, maintenance manuals, and comprehensive warranty coverage — ensuring your façade performs for decades.
